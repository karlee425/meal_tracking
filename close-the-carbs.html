Cooked yields
Every one of these is an assumption until you weigh it. Cooked weight ÷ raw or dry weight. Overwrite one and it stops being flagged.
Two worth knowing. Published ranges put cooked rice at ×2.5–3.0 of dry weight, so your ×2.9 sits near the top and ×3.14 sits outside it — the disputed value is the less likely one. And ×0.78 for potato is a roasting yield; a boiled potato barely loses weight, closer to ×1.0. If you boil and log cooked, ×0.78 credits you about 28% more carbohydrate than you actually ate.
